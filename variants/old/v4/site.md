# Site: Denisa Dragomir — alergare & coaching (denisadragomir.ro rebuild)

**Purpose:** Rebuild [denisadragomir.ro](https://denisadragomir.ro) around a **running coach** positioning (trail / mountain / ultramarathon), keeping the useful structure of the current WordPress site while tightening messaging toward coaching, plans, and trust.

**Audience:** Runners in România (și diaspora) who want structured training, trail and road goals, and guidance from an athlete-coach with proven race experience.

**Language:** Română (primary); optional EN snippets where useful for sponsors or international races.

**Build:** Static HTML lives in `site/public/` (generated by `scripts/build_site.py`). Stitch MCP was not available; pages follow `DESIGN.md` manually. Add a Stitch `projectId` to `.stitch/metadata.json` when you switch to Stitch-driven iterations.

---

## 1. Vision

- Warm, human, mountain-adjacent brand (not cold “gym app” UI).
- Proof-led: rezultate, media, concursuri = credibility.
- Conversion paths: planuri de antrenament, contact, testimoniale.
